About Instant Crew & This Policy

Instant Crew ("we", "us", or "our") operates an on-demand workforce matching platform that enables businesses ("Employers") to quickly fill urgent shifts with verified, flexible talent ("Applicants" or "Crew Members").

This Privacy Policy governs our web platform, Progressive Web Application (PWA), and all associated dispatch, verification, and payout services. It explains what information we collect from you, how it is processed, with whom it may be shared, and your legal rights under the Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (DPA).

Consent Notice

By creating an account, browsing shifts, posting requirements, or checking in to a scheduled shift, you acknowledge that you have read and consented to the practices described in this policy.

How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Real-Time Crew Matching: Calculating distance between available workers and urgent shift venues to ensure speedy arrival.
  • Attendance & Geofence Verification: Validating that confirmed crew members have physically arrived at the designated venue on time.
  • Instant Earnings & Payments: Initiating payouts to crew members upon shift completion and processing employer billing via GCash.
  • Trust & Safety: Verifying identities, detecting fraudulent accounts or duplicate registrations, preventing shift no-shows, and maintaining community standards.
  • Dispute Resolution: Using shift check-in logs and employer confirmations to quickly resolve payment or attendance disagreements.
  • Essential Communications: Sending urgent shift notifications, check-in reminders, schedule updates, and security verifications.
4

Location & GPS Tracking Transparency

Because Instant Crew specializes in rapid staffing, location data is crucial for workplace safety, attendance validation, and accurate dispatch. We prioritize your privacy with strict controls:

Our Shift-Only Location Commitment

We never track your location 24/7. Geolocation is requested only when: (1) you search for nearby shifts, (2) you are en-route to an accepted shift, or (3) you tap "Check In" at the venue within the employer's geofenced radius.

Once a shift has been checked out or cancelled, active GPS monitoring ceases immediately. You can revoke device location permissions at any time through your browser or device operating system settings, although doing so may prevent you from checking in to scheduled shifts.

Payments & GCash Data Processing

Instant Crew integrates with GCash and authorized payment gateways in the Philippines.

  • What we store: We record GCash account phone numbers and official transaction reference IDs to link payments with corresponding shifts and maintain historical receipts.
  • What we NEVER store: We never have access to, collect, or store your GCash MPIN, OTP (One-Time Password), biometric credentials, or bank card CVVs.
  • Disbursement Security: Payout disbursements are triggered automatically through verified API handshakes or verified administrator audits to ensure workers receive prompt compensation.
6

Data Sharing Between Users & Third Parties

To fulfill shifts, limited necessary data is shared between matched parties:

  • Shared with Employers: When an applicant is assigned or checks in, the employer can see their first name, profile picture, skill qualifications, arrival status, and rating. Direct private personal contact information is only shared when necessary for on-site shift coordination.
  • Shared with Applicants: When browsing or working shifts, applicants see the business name, job site address, map directions, supervisor contact number, attire guidelines, and pay rate.
  • Third-Party Service Providers: We utilize secure cloud infrastructure (hosting, real-time database sync, map rendering services). All vendors are bound by strict confidentiality agreements matching our privacy obligations.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose data if legally required by valid court subpoenas, the National Privacy Commission (NPC), or law enforcement agencies.
7

Data Security & Storage Safeguards

We implement industry-standard physical, organizational, and technical safeguards to shield your data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All communications between your device and our servers are encrypted in transit using 256-bit encryption.
  • Authentication Protection: Sessions are managed via secure, time-limited cryptographic tokens.
  • Access Restrictions: Customer records can only be accessed by authorized Instant Crew operations personnel under strict role-based permissions and audit logs.
8

Your Rights Under the Philippine DPA (RA 10173)

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, all registered users are recognized as data subjects and possess the following enforceable rights:

  • Right to be Informed: You have the right to know how your personal data is collected, handled, and stored.
  • Right to Access: You can request a summary or copy of your personal data held in our systems.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct or update any inaccurate or outdated information in your profile.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: You have the right to suspend, withdraw, or order the removal of your personal data if it is no longer necessary or has been unlawfully obtained.
  • Right to Damages: You may be indemnified for any damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, or unlawfully obtained personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may obtain an electronic copy of your information in an accessible format.
9

Cookies & Local Session Storage

Instant Crew uses modern browser storage mechanisms (Local Storage and Session Storage) rather than invasive tracking cookies. These store:

  • ic_worker_session: Keeps crew members signed in to view shift invitations without re-entering credentials.
  • ic_employer_session: Maintains employer portal authorization for shift posting.
  • Application state preferences, such as selected shift filters and active dashboard tabs.

We do not use cross-site tracking pixels or third-party behavioral advertising cookies.

10

Data Retention & Account Erasure

We retain personal records for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with staffing services. When an account is closed:

  • Profile details and credentials are deleted or permanently anonymized within 30 days.
  • Shift financial records, invoices, and GCash reference numbers may be retained for up to 5 years solely to comply with statutory taxation, accounting, and legal audit requirements in the Philippines.
