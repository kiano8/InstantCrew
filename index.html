Privacy Policy

Instant Crew • Data Protection & Security

Zero Ad Tracking

We never sell your personal data or shift records to advertisers.

Shift-Only GPS

Location is strictly requested for geofenced shift check-ins.

Safe GCash Payouts

Encrypted reference handling for instant disbursements.

1Information We Collect

To enable fast, on-demand staffing, Instant Crew collects:

  • Identity & Profile: Full name, mobile number, email, and ID verification.
  • For Applicants: Skill tags, past shift logs, employer feedback, and ratings.
  • For Employers: Business trade name, venue branches, and supervisor contacts.
  • Payment Records: GCash mobile numbers and official payout transaction references.

2Location & GPS Verification

Real-time GPS is collected solely to match nearby shifts and verify that confirmed crew members have arrived at the job site. We do not track you outside of active shifts.

3Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)

You have full rights to inspect, update, or request permanent deletion of your personal records. All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS 256-bit encryption.

4Questions & Privacy Requests

Contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@instantcrew.ph or message us directly on Facebook.

Terms of Service

Instant Crew • Simple Shift & Payout Guidelines

Fast Payouts

Prompt disbursements directly to your GCash account upon shift completion.

Punctual Check-In

Arrive on time and check in within the employer's GPS venue geofence.

Safe & Fair Work

Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or unsafe work environments.

1Platform Role

Instant Crew connects businesses with independent flexible workers for short-notice hourly shifts. Workers choose when and where they work on a shift-by-shift basis.

2Crew Member Commitments

  • Arrive 10–15 minutes early and check in via GPS at the venue.
  • Wear the specified work attire (e.g. non-slip shoes, appropriate uniform).
  • Perform duties diligently and maintain honest, professional workplace conduct.

3Employer Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate job locations, supervisor contacts, and a safe work environment.
  • Sign off on verified shift hours promptly to enable instant worker payouts.
  • Pay the full agreed hourly rate without unauthorized deductions.

4Cancellations & Disputes

Reliability is vital. Unnotified no-shows or repeated late cancellations harm community trust and may lead to account penalties. In case of discrepancies, our support team audits GPS timestamps for a fair resolution.
