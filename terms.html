Overview & Agreement

Welcome to Instant Crew. These Terms of Service ("Terms") constitute a simple, binding agreement between you (as an "Employer" or "Applicant / Crew Member") and Instant Crew.

By registering, logging in, posting shifts, applying for work, or checking in to a scheduled shift, you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree with any part, please refrain from using our services.

2

What Instant Crew Does

Instant Crew is an on-demand workforce marketplace. We provide the technology that connects businesses needing short-notice, hourly staff (kitchen staff, event crew, baristas, logistics, warehouse helpers) with available, qualified workers.

Independent Crew Status

Crew members choose when and where they work on a shift-by-shift basis. Using Instant Crew does not create an employer-employee relationship with Instant Crew itself.

3

Crew Member Commitments

When you accept a shift on Instant Crew, you agree to:

  • Punctuality: Arrive at the job venue at least 10–15 minutes before the shift start time.
  • Check-In Verification: Use the Instant Crew app to tap "Check In" once you are physically present at the site.
  • Dress Code & Presentation: Follow required workplace attire (e.g. black pants, non-slip shoes, hairnet) as specified by the employer.
  • Professionalism: Perform duties honestly and diligently, following the supervisor's instructions.
  • Honesty: Never falsely manipulate GPS coordinates or claim unworked hours.
4

Employer Responsibilities

When posting shifts and hiring crew, employers agree to:

  • Accurate Details: Provide exact venue address, working hours, supervisor contact, and clear role expectations.
  • Safe Environment: Provide a hazard-free workplace compliant with Philippine labor safety standards.
  • Respect & Non-Discrimination: Treat all crew members fairly and professionally.
  • Timely Confirmation: Sign off on completed shift hours promptly so worker payouts can be disbursed without delay.
  • Pay Rates: Pay the hourly rate advertised in the shift posting. No unauthorized deductions are allowed.
5

Rates, Payments & GCash Payouts

We prioritize fast, transparent earnings:

  • Hourly Pay: Shift compensation is calculated based on verified check-in and check-out times.
  • GCash Disbursements: Payouts are transferred directly to the crew member's registered GCash account after shift sign-off.
  • Fees: Any service fees or payment processing costs are clearly shown before confirmation.
6

Attendance & GPS Geofencing

Instant Crew uses real-time GPS verification during active shift hours to confirm attendance:

Venue Geofence

Your check-in is verified when your device is within the employer's venue radius. GPS is only active during shift check-ins and scheduled hours.

7

Cancellations & No-Shows

Because businesses rely on urgent staffing, reliability is essential:

  • Worker Cancellations: If an emergency arises, workers must cancel through the app as early as possible so a replacement can be found.
  • 1-Hour Arrival Limit: Once an employer confirms a booking, the crew member has 1 hour to arrive on site and tap Mark Arrived in the app. For shifts scheduled later, the hour starts at the scheduled start time.
  • Automatic Cancellation: If arrival is not marked within the 1-hour limit, the shift is automatically cancelled and recorded as a no-show. Both the employer and the crew member are notified right away.
  • Rating Deduction: Each no-show lowers the crew member's rating by 0.20 stars.
  • 5-Day Suspension: A crew member with more than 3 no-shows is suspended for 5 days and cannot accept shifts until the suspension ends.
  • Permanent Deactivation: After a suspension has been served, any further no-show permanently deactivates the account.
  • Employer Cancellations: If an employer cancels a shift shortly before the start time, the assigned worker may be eligible for a cancellation stipend.
8

Workplace Safety & Conduct

Instant Crew has a zero-tolerance policy for:

  • Harassment, bullying, verbal abuse, or physical intimidation.
  • Theft, property damage, or unauthorized use of employer equipment.
  • Working while under the influence of alcohol or prohibited substances.

Account Status & Disputes

If a dispute arises regarding attendance, shift hours, or payment, our support team reviews GPS timestamps, check-in records, and employer confirmations to ensure fair resolution.

Instant Crew reserves the right to suspend or close accounts that violate these Terms or engage in fraudulent behavior.
